Common questions

What Azure services do you provide?

We help with Azure setup and architecture, migration, security and compliance reviews, backup and disaster recovery, cost optimization and 24/7 managed support. You can take one service or combine them.

Can you support an Azure environment we already run?

Yes. We start with a review of your current subscriptions and workloads, then agree what we will monitor and support. The scope is confirmed in a written proposal.

Can you migrate our on-premises servers or another cloud to Azure?

Yes. We handle migrations from on-premises data centers and from other cloud platforms, after assessing your applications and dependencies.

How long does a migration or setup take?

It depends on the number of servers and applications, data volume and how complex your setup is. We give you a timeline after we understand your environment.

Will my team face downtime?

We plan changes in stages to keep disruption low, and we agree the cutover plan with you in advance.

Can you help reduce our Azure bill?

Yes. We review your usage and recommend changes such as right-sizing and removing unused resources. Savings depend on your environment, so we do not promise a fixed figure before reviewing it.

What does managed support include?

Ongoing monitoring and support for your Azure environment. The exact scope is confirmed in a written proposal before you commit.
